countries.json
- Simplified Natural Earth low-resolution country outlines via the GeoPandas
  demonstration dataset; vector geometry repackaged as compact JSON.
- Natural Earth public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Country boundaries are NOT authoritative legal border determinations.
- This dataset is simplified and not adequate for cadastral, legal, or
  high-resolution admin-1/admin-2 needs.
